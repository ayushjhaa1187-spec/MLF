Week 10: High-Yield Summary

Duality, KKT Conditions & Optimization in ML • Core Formulas, Definitions & Quick Code

Weak vs Strong Duality
Weak duality ($d^* \le p^*$) holds always; Strong duality ($d^* = p^*$) holds under Slater's condition for convex problems.
KKT Conditions
Primal feasibility, Dual feasibility, Complementary slackness ($\lambda_i f_i = 0$), Stationarity ($\nabla_x \mathcal{L} = 0$).
Support Vectors
Data points where $y_i(w^Tx_i+b) = 1$ with non-zero dual variables $\alpha_i > 0$.
