Orthogonal Subspace Projection & The Error Vector

Practice Week 3 Week 3 Textbook

Consider the subspace $S = C(A) \subset \mathbb{R}^3$ spanned by the standard canonical basis vectors of the $xy$-plane: $A = \begin{bmatrix} a_1 & a_2 \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 \\ 0 & 0 \end{bmatrix}$. The orthogonal projection matrix is $P = A(A^T A)^{-1}A^T = \text{diag}(1, 1, 0)$. Adjust observation vector $b = [b_x, b_y, b_z]^T$ below. Notice that the projection $p = Pb$ remains confined to the plane, while the residual error $e = b - p$ is strictly perpendicular to every column of $A$ ($a_1^T e = 0$ and $a_2^T e = 0$).

Gradient Descent & Learning Rate Dynamics

Practice Week 8 Week 8 Textbook

Simulate gradient descent minimization on the anisotropic quadratic bowl $f(x, y) = x^2 + 3y^2$ with gradient $\nabla f(x, y) = [2x, 6y]^T$. The maximum eigenvalue of the Hessian is $L = \lambda_{\max}(H) = 6$. Theoretical convergence requires $\eta < \frac{2}{L} = \frac{1}{3} \approx 0.333$. Experiment with values of $\eta$ to observe smooth convergence, zigzag oscillations, and divergence.
