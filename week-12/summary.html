Week 12: High-Yield Summary

Multivariate Gaussians, GMMs, EM Algorithm & Limit Theorems • Core Formulas, Definitions & Quick Code

Multivariate Gaussian
Defined by mean vector $\mu$ and covariance matrix $\Sigma$. Density contours form ellipsoids.
EM Algorithm
Iteratively alternates between computing soft responsibilities (E-step) and updating mixture parameters (M-step). Monotonically increases likelihood.
Central Limit Theorem
Sample averages of i.i.d. variables become asymptotically Gaussian regardless of underlying distribution.
