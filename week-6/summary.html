Singular Value Decomposition (SVD) & Positive Definiteness • Core Formulas, Definitions & Quick Code

SVD Factorization
$A = U \Sigma V^T$. Applies to any $m \times n$ matrix.
Positive Definite Matrices
$x^T A x > 0 \iff \lambda_i > 0$. Crucial for convex loss curvature and Hessians.
Eckart-Young Theorem
Top $k$ singular components form the optimal rank-$k$ approximation.
