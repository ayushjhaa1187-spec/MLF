Week 7: High-Yield Summary

Principal Component Analysis (PCA) & Dimensionality Reduction • Core Formulas, Definitions & Quick Code

Variance Maximization
Maximizing projected variance leads directly to the eigenvalue equation $\Sigma u = \lambda u$.
Covariance Eigendecomposition
Principal components are the eigenvectors of the centered sample covariance matrix $\Sigma = \frac{1}{N}X^TX$.
Reconstruction Equivalence
Maximizing preserved variance is mathematically identical to minimizing $\ell_2$ reconstruction error.
