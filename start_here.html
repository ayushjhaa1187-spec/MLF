2. Mathematical Notation Guide

Symbol / TypographyMeaningStandard FormExample
Lowercase italic ($x, \alpha$)Scalar quantity$\mathbb{R}$$c = 2.5, \; \lambda = 0.01$
Lowercase bold ($x, v, b$)Column vector$\mathbb{R}^{d \times 1}$$v = [v_1, v_2, \dots, v_d]^T$
Uppercase bold ($A, X, \Sigma$)Matrix$\mathbb{R}^{m \times n}$$X \in \mathbb{R}^{N \times D}$ (Design matrix)
$\langle u, v \rangle$ or $u^T v$Standard Euclidean inner productScalar$\sum_{i=1}^d u_i v_i$
$\|v\|_2 = \sqrt{v^T v}$$\ell_2$ Euclidean vector normScalar $\ge 0$$\|v\|_2 = \sqrt{\sum v_i^2}$
$\nabla f(x)$Gradient vector of scalar function$\mathbb{R}^d$$[\partial f / \partial x_1, \dots, \partial f / \partial x_d]^T$
$H$ or $\nabla^2 f(x)$Hessian matrix of second derivatives$\mathbb{R}^{d \times d}$$H_{ij} = \partial^2 f / \partial x_i \partial x_j$
$P$Orthogonal projection matrix$\mathbb{R}^{m \times m}$$P = A(A^TA)^{-1}A^T, \; P^T = P = P^2$
