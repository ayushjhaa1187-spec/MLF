Week 2: High-Yield Summary

Calculus for ML: Sets, Functions & Derivatives • Core Formulas, Definitions & Quick Code

Gradient Vector
$\nabla f(x) = [\partial f/\partial x_1, \dots, \partial f/\partial x_d]^T$. Points in direction of steepest ascent.
Directional Derivative
$D_v f(x) = \nabla f(x)^T v$. Maximum when $v \parallel \nabla f(x)$.
Affine Tangent Plane
$f(x) \approx f(x_0) + \nabla f(x_0)^T (x - x_0)$ provides local first-order linear approximation.
